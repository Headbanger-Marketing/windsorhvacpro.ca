About Us

Windsor's Trusted Hands for Heating & Cooling

From century homes in Sandwich to ranch styles in Forest Glade, we keep Essex County comfortable through our humid lake-effect summers and the biting cold of a Windsor January.

Our Story

Rooted in the Border City

Windsor HVAC Pro was started by neighbours, for neighbours, a locally owned company built around the realities of living and working on the Detroit River.

We're based right on Jefferson Blvd, minutes from the Ambassador Bridge, and we know this city the way only locals do. We've crawled through hundred-year-old houses in Olde Walkerville, swapped furnaces in 1970s raised ranches across the east end, and installed ductless mini-splits in new builds from Tecumseh to LaSalle. Windsor's housing stock is one of the most varied in southwestern Ontario, and there's barely a system we haven't worked on.

Living on the Essex County peninsula means weather that swings hard in both directions. Our summers pull warm, humid air up from Lake Erie and Lake St. Clair, while winter delivers deep cold and wind off the lakes that pushes older furnaces to their limit. That's the climate we design every repair, install, and tune-up around, not a generic Ontario average, but the actual conditions outside your front door.

As a family-run business, we treat every call the way we'd want our own family treated: straightforward pricing, clear explanations, and work that holds up. When you call Windsor HVAC Pro, you're not reaching a national call centre or a chain, you're getting the same team your neighbours in Amherstburg, Belle River, Leamington, and Lakeshore have trusted for years.

Why Windsor Homeowners Call Us First

We understand the quirks of Windsor-Essex homes, the aging ductwork in Walkerville, the tight mechanical rooms in east-end raised ranches, the lake-effect humidity that strains air conditioners every July. That local knowledge means faster diagnoses, smarter recommendations, and comfort that actually holds up through every season on the peninsula.

Our Commitment

Our Commitment to Essex County

Every furnace we install, every air conditioner we service, and every duct we clean is held to the standard we'd set for our own homes. That means sizing equipment properly for your space rather than oversizing to make a sale, and walking you through the real trade-offs between heat pumps, central air, and ductless systems so you choose what fits your home, your budget, and your comfort goals.

Our work is backed by licensed, insured technicians who live and work in Windsor-Essex. Whether it's a no-heat call on a January night or a spring AC tune-up before the humidity settles in, our goal is the same: diagnose accurately, fix it properly the first time, and leave your home cleaner than we found it.
